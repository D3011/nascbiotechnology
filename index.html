Our Department

Rooted in Science,
Built for Tomorrow

Established in 2002, we've grown into a full Research Department offering Ph.D and M.Phil guidance under Bharathiar University — blending classroom rigor with hands-on research.

🔬
State-of-the-Art Laboratories
DNA Sequencer, PCR Machine, CO₂ Incubator, Bioreactor, Gel Documentation — labs open till 6 PM daily.
🌿
Greenhouse & Medicinal Garden
Live research environment for plant propagation, extraction, and applied botany studies.
📡
National Conferences & INSA Workshops
Hosts DST-INSPIRE camps, State and National level scientific conferences throughout the year.
