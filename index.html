Our Department

Rooted in Science,
Built for Tomorrow

The Department of Biotechnology was established in 2002 with both UG and PG courses. Upgraded to a Research Department in 2015, offering Ph.D guidance under Bharathiar University. We blend rigorous academics with hands-on research and industry exposure to shape competent biotechnologists.

🔬
State-of-the-Art Laboratories
DNA Sequencer, PCR Machine, CO₂ Incubator, Bioreactor, Gel Documentation and more — open till 6 PM daily.
🌿
Greenhouse & Medicinal Garden
Live research environment for plant propagation, extraction and applied botany studies.
🏛️
National Conferences & INSA Workshops
Regular DST-INSPIRE Camps and State / National level scientific knowledge-sharing platforms.

Career & Industry Scope

What Can You Do with a Biotechnology Degree?

Biotechnology is one of the fastest-growing fields of the 21st century. Our graduates contribute across medicine, agriculture, industry, environment and research.

💊
Pharmaceutical & Healthcare
Drug discovery, vaccine development, clinical research and diagnostics with top pharma companies.
🧬
Genomics & Molecular Biology
Genetic engineering, DNA sequencing, bioinformatics and personalized medicine at global research labs.
🌾
Agricultural Biotechnology
Improved crops, biopesticides and biofertilizers for sustainable, food-secure agriculture.
🏭
Industrial & Fermentation
Enzyme production, biofuels, fermentation technology and quality control in bio-manufacturing.
🌊
Environmental Biotechnology
Bioremediation, waste treatment, pollution control and green energy solutions.
🔬
Research & Academia
Ph.D. or postdoctoral research at national labs, IITs, IISc and international universities.
🧪
Clinical & Medical Labs
Medical Lab Technologist, Microbiologist or Pathology assistant in hospitals and diagnostic labs.
💻
Bioinformatics & IT
Pharmaceutical IT, clinical data management and research software development.
